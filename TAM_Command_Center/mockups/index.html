TAM Command Center: design mockups, round 2

Static mockups with sample data (Tue 13 Oct 2026, 14:05, 68 items in the queue). Nothing here is built into the app. All three Today options use the PM tier order (overdue/critical, due today, alerts, waiting, upgrades, upcoming) and move Upcoming, Recently changed and Account load out of the main view. Pages are interactive where noted (filters, drawer, selection).

Today: three directions
Recommended

A. Focus Deck

Shown: a six-chip summary strip that filters the list (Plan 11, Overdue/critical 6, Due today 5, Alerts 7, Waiting 14, Upgrades 8, Upcoming 28), one large "Do this first" card, the next six items numbered 2 to 7 in order, a daily progress ring (3 of 14 done), and a "Show everything (68)" link. Hidden: items 8 and later of the plan (one expander), every non-plan tier until its chip is clicked, and Upcoming, Recently changed and Account load, which live in a right-hand drawer opened from three quiet buttons. The calmest option and the closest to the current build: same data, far less on screen.

Open A

B. Day Timeline

Shown: a read-only count strip with today's plan highlighted, a suggested afternoon agenda from now to 18:00 with a NOW marker, estimated durations and the plan items placed in tier order, what was already done this morning, and a "do first" card with three steps. Hidden: items that do not fit before 18:00 (listed small as "does not fit today"), all non-plan tiers (counts only), and the three Later lists behind buttons. Best at making the day feel planned, but it needs a new estimated-duration field and a scheduling rule that the spec does not have yet.

Open B

C. Triage Split

Shown: a segmented summary (the same six groups plus Plan) that switches the list, a compact numbered list of seven items on the left, and a detail pane on the right with "What to do" steps, "Why it is here" and key facts for the selected item. Arrow keys move the selection. Hidden: everything beyond seven rows, and Upcoming, Recently changed and Account load in a collapsed "Later" tab bar under the split. Best at "know exactly what to do", but the step text needs a next-action field or a convention to be real.
