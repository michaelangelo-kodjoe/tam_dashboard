Overview

Change. A Quarter Has Three Upgrade Dates: One DEV, One East PROD, One West PROD. The Timeline Shows One Marker Per Date And Polarity. Colour Is The Only Distinction: West = Accent Orange, East = Info Blue, DEV = New Purple Token (--purple, #b48cff, Proposed), Unknown PROD = Neutral Grey. No Text Tags; A Compact Colour Legend (West, East, DEV) Sits Under The Timeline And The Narrow List; Unknown PROD Grey Has No Legend Entry. Each Marker Has A Tooltip And A Screen-Reader Label. Two Polarities On One Day Sit Side By Side Under One Date.
